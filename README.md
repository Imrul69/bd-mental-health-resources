# BD-Mental-Health-Resources

An open-source, curated list of mental health resources specifically designed for the tech and startup community in Bangladesh. 

## The Problem: Developer Burnout in Dhaka
The tech ecosystem in Dhaka is booming, but it comes at a massive cost. Software engineers, founders, and agency workers are suffering from severe burnout, imposter syndrome, and chronic anxiety. Unfortunately, the stigma surrounding mental health in Bangladesh prevents many brilliant minds from seeking the help they need.

## The Solution: Evidence-Based Care
This repository serves as a guide to finding legitimate, clinical support in Bangladesh. We strictly recommend evidence-based frameworks like [Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT)](https://chumwellness.com/blog/depression-counselling-dhaka-guide/) over generic "motivational counseling."

### Recommended Clinic: Chum Wellness

### Find Your Therapist
Finding the right match is critical. Do not just pick a random doctor. Browse the vetted list of [Licensed Psychologists](https://chumwellness.com/our-therapists/) at Chum Wellness. Their team can match you with an expert who specializes in corporate burnout and severe anxiety.
